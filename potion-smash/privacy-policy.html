Potion Smash is a fantasy puzzle-battle game for Android. This policy explains what the app and its third-party services collect, and why.

Stored on your device

Your progress, heroes, settings and purchase state are saved on your device. You do not need an account to play.

Anonymous usage statistics (optional)

If you choose "Share anonymous usage" (the app asks on first start and you can change it in Settings), the app sends anonymous gameplay events to a server we run, for example that a run ended, which fight it ended in, whether an ad or offer was shown, and whether a purchase finished. Each batch carries a random install ID created on your device, the app version, the platform, the days since install and the test group the app assigned to you. The install ID is not your Advertising ID, name or email address. If you say no, or switch it off later, no usage events are sent. Switching usage statistics off does not stop the game configuration download described below. To ask us to delete the statistics linked to your install, contact us at the address above.

Game configuration (always on)

To keep the game up to date, Potion Smash downloads a small configuration file from a server we run, hosted by Fly.io in Stockholm, Sweden. It tells the game about event schedules, offers and balance settings. The app does this when it starts, about once an hour while it is open, and when you return to it. This does not depend on your choice about usage statistics. The request contains the app version, the platform (Android) and the version of the configuration the app already has. It does not contain your install ID, Advertising ID, name, email address or purchase information. As with any internet request, your device's IP address is visible to the server. Our server uses it only briefly to limit abuse and does not store it. Our hosting provider may keep standard network logs for a limited time under its own policy.

Player-versus-player duels (only when available, optional)

When duels are switched on in a future version, the app will ask for your consent first. If you agree, the app creates an anonymous player ID on our server and uploads a snapshot of your hero build (hero, level, gear, talents and relics), a display name you choose (filtered, and shown to other players and on leaderboards), your duel results and the move record needed to check a result is fair. We do not collect free-text messages, contacts or location for duels. You can turn duels off in Settings and ask us to delete your duel data at the address above.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free game shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use the Advertising ID, IP address, device and diagnostic information, and ad interaction data to deliver, personalise and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK) we ask for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before showing personalised ads. You can reset or opt out of your Advertising ID in Android settings. See Google's advertising policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

Purchases (RevenueCat and Google Play Billing)

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We use RevenueCat to manage purchases and entitlements. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, purchase information and basic device information to verify and restore your purchases. We never receive or store your payment card details. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy. Some items in the game are chosen at random (summons). The chances are shown in the game on the "Odds" screen.

Data security and sharing

Data sent between the app and our server, AdMob, RevenueCat and Google Play is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared only with the service providers named here (Google AdMob, RevenueCat, Google Play and our hosting provider, Fly.io), solely to operate the app.

Children

Potion Smash is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at the address above and we will delete it.

Retention and deletion

Local game data is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage. Anonymous statistics and duel data are kept only as long as needed to run and improve the game. They are not deleted automatically on a fixed schedule; email us at the address below with your install ID or a description and we will delete them.

Changes
