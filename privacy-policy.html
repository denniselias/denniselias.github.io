The Magic Library is an interactive story and puzzle game for Android. This policy explains what information the app and its third-party services collect and how it is used. The app contains mature themes (18+).

Information stored on your device

Your progress, settings, coins, stars and purchases state are stored locally on your device. This data is not sent to us. We do not ask you to create an account.

Anonymous usage statistics (optional)

If you choose "Share anonymous usage" (when the app first asks, or later in Settings), the app sends anonymous usage events to a server we run, hosted on Fly.io. These events describe what happens in the game, for example that a puzzle was completed, how many taps it took, which screens were opened, and whether a shop offer was shown or a purchase finished. Each batch carries a random install ID created on your device, the app version, the platform (Android), the number of days since install and the test group the app assigned to you. The install ID is not your device ID, Advertising ID, name or email address. We never collect your story choices, free text, payment details or purchase receipts.

We use these statistics only to understand how the game is played and to improve it, for example to balance puzzle difficulty. If you say no, or switch the setting off later, nothing is kept and no usage events are sent. To ask us to delete the statistics linked to your install, email us at the address below.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use the Advertising ID, IP address, device and diagnostic information (such as crash logs and performance data), and ad interaction data to deliver, personalize and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK) we ask for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before showing personalized ads. You can reset or opt out of your Advertising ID in your Android settings. See Google's advertising policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

Purchases (RevenueCat and Google Play Billing)

In-app purchases, such as removing ads, are processed by Google Play. We use RevenueCat to manage purchases and entitlements. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, purchase and subscription information, and basic device information to verify and restore your purchases. We never receive or store your payment card details. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Data security

Data sent between the app and AdMob, RevenueCat and Google Play is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared only with the service providers described above (Google AdMob, RevenueCat, Google Play and our hosting provider Fly.io), solely to operate the app.

Children

This app is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and deletion

Local game data is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage. For requests about anonymous usage statistics, or data held by AdMob or RevenueCat, contact us at the email above or those providers directly.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest revision.
